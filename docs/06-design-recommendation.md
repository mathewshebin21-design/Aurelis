# 06 · Design comparison and recommendation

| | **A · Institutional Terminal** | **B · Private Wealth** | **C · Quant Research Lab** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual language | Charcoal/graphite, electric-blue accent, monospace figures, ticker tape, icon rail, status bar | Ivory and midnight navy, champagne-gold hairlines, Newsreader serif display, generous whitespace | White and graphite, IBM Plex Sans/Mono, module IDs, grid-paper hero, sidebar module tree |
| Density | Highest: 6–8 panels per screen | Lowest: one idea per section | Medium-high, modular cards |
| Best at | Speed and scanning for power users | Narrative, committee memos, client-facing reports | Showing analytical rigour clearly; long sessions |
| Weaknesses | Dark + dense is harder to read in screenshots and on projectors; intimidating to non-specialists | Less efficient for heavy modelling; more scrolling | Less distinctive at first glance than A or B |
| Portfolio signal | "Can build pro tools" | "Understands clients and communication" | "Rigorous, reproducible, engineering-minded analyst" |

## Recommendation: **Design C, with B's editorial layer for reports**

1. **Readable for reviewers.** Recruiters and hiring managers look at screenshots, demos and slides; C's light, structured layout stays legible in all three, and in a case study.
2. **Fits the product thesis.** Module IDs, a visible engine status, a methodology for every score and the formula-first landing page all reinforce "the engine calculates, AI explains."
3. **Scales to the full feature set.** The sidebar module tree has room for peers, portfolios, risk and saved sessions without redesign.
4. **Keeps the best of the others.** Because components read semantic tokens, B's serif memo style can become the **investment committee report export**, and A's palette can ship as an optional **dark terminal theme** at low cost.

Alternative choices are fully viable: pick **A** if the target audience is trading-floor or hedge-fund roles; pick **B** if it is private banking, wealth management or consulting.
